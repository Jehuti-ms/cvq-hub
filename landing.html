The problem

Move from paper to proof

CVQ programmes live and die by their records. The old way is slow, lossy, and impossible to audit. CVQ Hub is built for how teachers actually work.

📄

The old way

  • Sessions written on paper, lost in the shuffle
  • Evidence photos scattered across different phones
  • Hand-made reports take hours to compile
  • No way for coordinators to see real activity
  • Data trapped on one device or one computer
  • Impossible to prove hours delivered at review time
✨

The CVQ Hub way

  • Sessions logged on your phone in seconds
  • Photo & video evidence captured directly to Drive
  • Printable reports generated with one tap
  • Live coordinator dashboard across all teachers
  • Data stored securely in your school's Google account
  • Every hour, session, and mark permanently recorded
Two views, one source of truth

Built for trainees and teachers

Every session updates both the trainee's progress record and your own effort record. No double entry.

👨‍🎓

Trainee Progress

See what every trainee has covered, what they've achieved, and where they need more practice.

  • Session-by-session log with topics covered
  • Evidence captured on-site (photo / video)
  • Marks & assessments per unit and topic
  • Attendance with late / absent / excused states
  • Progress chart across the whole semester
👩‍🏫

Teacher Effort

Prove the hours you've delivered, the sessions you've run, and the evidence you've collected.

  • Total hours & sessions per week / month
  • Time broken down by class & unit
  • Evidence submissions with timestamps
  • Attendance marking completion rates
  • Printable effort summary for reviews
